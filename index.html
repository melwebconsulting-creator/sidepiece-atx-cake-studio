OUR PHILOSOPHY

GOOD THINGS TAKE THE REAL STUFF.

We believe the best cakes begin with honest ingredients and a little imagination. From homemade jams and syrups to colors made in-house, every detail is part of the art.

REAL INGREDIENTS

Never cutting corners. We select organic staples, real cream, pure butter, fresh seasonal fruits, and whole spices that taste as vibrant as they look.

MADE FROM SCRATCH

From slow-simmered berry preserves and infused syrups to natural dyes coaxed from flowers and berries, our components are crafted by hand in our studio.

NEVER COOKIE-CUTTER

No factory duplicates, no rigid catalog presets. Every cake is treated as an individual piece of sculpture crafted specifically for the person celebrating.

HOW IT WORKS

YOUR IDEA. OUR LITTLE OBSESSION.

Creating a one-of-a-kind cake is a collaborative artistic journey. Here is how we turn your celebration into something sweet.

01

DREAM IT

Tell us about your celebration, ideas, and inspiration. Whether it's a moodboard, a color obsession, or a favorite vintage era, share what makes your heart sing.

02

DESIGN IT

Discuss the colors, mood, and details you'd love. We collaborate on flavor pairings, homemade jam fillings, and custom artistic piping suited to your venue.

03

MAKE IT SWEET

Reach out to discuss your custom cake. Inquiries and custom pricing are handled directly through direct messages to ensure personal attention for every event.

MEET THE ARTIST

THE HAND BEHIND THE BUTTERCREAM.

Sidepiece ATX is a creative cake practice led by Ella Tasso Adams, where real ingredients meet a playful artistic spirit. The goal is simple: make cakes that feel personal, expressive, and impossible to copy exactly.

Rooted in Austin's independent creative community, each bake is guided by the belief that custom food should be as thoughtful as art. No rigid molds, no artificial shortcuts—just genuine craft, vibrant house-made dyes, and a passion for edible celebration.
